# Making the Merge: rules and card list (v3)

## Rules
- 12-card decks. 6 turns. 3 locations, revealed on turns 1, 2, 3.
- Fire (energy) = turn number. Start with 3 cards, draw 1 each turn (hand max 7).
- Both players play secretly; the player winning more locations reveals first.
- Max 4 cards per side at a location. Win 2 of 3 locations; total influence breaks a split.
- Stakes start at 1 jury vote. Playing your idol doubles them from next turn; the final turn doubles them again. Quitting loses the current stakes only.
- Ranks: Castaway 0, Swing Vote 10, Merged 25, Juror 45, Finalist 70, Sole Survivor 100.

## Collection
- Everyone starts with the 12-card starter deck and 100 rice (one free Tree Mail).
- Rice comes from wins only: a flat 20 per win. Losses, ties and quitting pay 0.
- Tree Mail costs 100 rice and holds 3 cards. The first slot is always a card you do not own yet, when one is available. A card you already own is traded back for 10 rice; nobody holds two copies until variants are released. When every card in your unlocked tiers is found, Tree Mail closes until you rank up.
- Pace target: 5 wins per Tree Mail (about 10 matches at a 50% win rate).
- Pull weights by tier: Day One 10, Pre-Merge 7, The Merge 5, Legends 3.
- Tiers unlock permanently at your highest rank: Pre-Merge at Swing Vote (10), The Merge at Merged (25), Legends at Juror (45).
- Each owned card records a count and a variants list, ready for rare variants later.
- Art is shared by everyone; only editors of the artifact can upload it. Collections are per person (account when available, otherwise device).

## Locations
- **Tribal Council**: End of each turn: if 2+ cards are here, the one with the least influence is voted out.
- **Exile Island**: Each player can have only 1 card here.
- **Immunity Challenge**: Cards here can't be voted out, moved or flipped.
- **Reward Feast**: Cards played here get +2 influence.
- **Hidden Idol Spot**: The first card each player plays here gets a hidden idol.
- **Swap Beach**: After turn 4, every card here switches sides.
- **Edge of Extinction**: Cards voted out at other locations come back here, if there's room.
- **Loved Ones Visit**: When you play a card here, draw a card.
- **The Merge**: When revealed, both players draw 2 cards.
- **Fire-Making**: Each player can have only 2 cards here.
- **Day 1 Camp**: No effect. Build a shelter.
- **Island of the Idols**: End of each turn: cards here get +1 influence.
